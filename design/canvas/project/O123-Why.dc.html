Arms and back are your focus.
Push / Pull / Legs + Upper / Lower, five days: every muscle is trained about twice a week.

Extra sets for arms and back, and they are the last thing to be cut when time is short.
Lower back: nothing risky for it is in the plan, and gentler options were picked first.
Lighter leg work, with extra calf and inner-thigh work, to leave room for your running.
Trimmed to fit 60 minutes: the most important lifts stayed.
